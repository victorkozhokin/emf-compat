# EMF Compat: Gliders — Changelog

## 2.0.0

### Added

- Added a Fabric 1.21.1 build (Gliders and Reliable Gliders)

### Dependencies

- Requires Entity Model Features 3.3.2 and EMF Compat Core 2.0.0

## 1.0.0

### Added

- First release
- Gliding poses stay visible instead of being overwritten by your resource pack
- Supports Paragliders, Gliders and Reliable Gliders, each with its own toggle
- Your pack's flight animation plays while you glide, with any of the three mods
- The glider's opening animation now plays in full — your legs swing under it before you settle into flight
- Head tracking keeps working while gliding
